Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how INDYpendent Bytes (“we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, and protects information when you use our website, Resource Discovery Agent, intake forms, and related services (the “Services”).

1. Information We Collect

We may collect:

  • Information you provide — such as name, email, location, role (e.g., cultivator or land host), goals, constraints, and other details you submit through intake forms, the Resource Discovery Agent, or contact channels.
  • Account information — if you create an account, identifiers and profile details associated with that account.
  • Usage information — such as pages viewed, queries submitted to the agent, device/browser type, and approximate location derived from IP address.
  • Cookies and similar technologies — used for basic site function, preferences, and understanding how the Services are used.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Operate and improve the Services, including resource matching and pathway guidance
  • Respond to intake submissions and inquiries
  • Coordinate appropriate next steps with partners when you request that
  • Maintain security, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot issues
  • Meet legal obligations
  • Send service-related communications (and optional updates if you opt in)

3. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information:

  • With service providers who help us operate the Services (e.g., hosting, forms, analytics), under obligations to protect your data
  • With partner organizations when needed to fulfill a request you made (for example, connecting you to a relevant program), and only as appropriate for that purpose
  • When required by law, or to protect rights, safety, and security
  • In connection with a reorganization or transfer of assets, with appropriate safeguards

4. Data Retention

We retain information only as long as needed for the purposes described in this Policy, unless a longer period is required by law. Intake and progress records may be kept so we can support follow-up and improve coordination quality.

5. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Choices

  • You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you, subject to legal limits.
  • You may opt out of non-essential communications.
  • You can control cookies through your browser settings; some features may not work if cookies are disabled.

7. Children’s Privacy

The Services are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have done so, contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

8. Third-Party Services

The Services may link to or integrate third-party tools (for example, form providers, authentication providers, or external program sites). Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will reflect the latest revision. Continued use of the Services after changes means you acknowledge the updated Policy.

10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact: indypendentbytes@gmail.com
