## What This Source Provides

The handbook explains:

- how and when to file an Indiana unemployment-insurance claim;
- information and documents needed to apply;
- Uplink Claimant Self-Service requirements;
- identity verification;
- Indiana Career Connect registration;
- WorkOne and RESEA requirements;
- monetary and nonmonetary eligibility concepts;
- weekly vouchers and work-search records;
- partial benefits and benefit calculations;
- overpayments and fraud consequences;
- appeals and administrative-law-judge hearings; and
- claimant contact and language-access information.

## Key Engagement Guidance

When recommending this source, the agent should tell users to prepare:

- a valid email address;
- Social Security number;
- government-issued identification;
- complete employment history for the prior two years;
- dates worked and reasons each job ended;
- recent pay stubs;
- information about severance, PTO payouts, pensions, retirement, workers’ compensation, or other income;
- out-of-state employment information when applicable; and
- records of weekly work-search activity.

## Important User Actions

The handbook states that users should:

1. File promptly after becoming unemployed.
2. Monitor Uplink Claimant Self-Service for correspondence and document requests.
3. Register with Indiana Career Connect when required.
4. Complete identity verification.
5. File weekly vouchers on time.
6. Maintain verifiable work-search records.
7. Attend required WorkOne or RESEA appointments.
8. Appeal within the deadline shown on a determination when they disagree.
